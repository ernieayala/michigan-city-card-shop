# What Sells in 2026: TCG or Sports, Slabs or Raw

> Last updated: 2026-10-04
> Scope: which part of the card market is worth a small dealer's money and time
> in late 2026: trading card games against sports cards, graded cards (slabs)
> against raw, and what turns over fastest. Every figure was retrieved
> 2026-10-04 unless marked.

## Summary

Both trading card games and sports cards are at record dollar volume, but the
growth sits in different places. Sports cards are the larger pool of single
card sales on eBay ($1.36B in the first half of 2026), driven by vintage,
one-of-ones and star rookies; modern base and mid-tier sports cards, the cards
a small shop handles most, are oversupplied and softening. Trading card games
are growing about twice as fast and take about 70% of all grading. Pokémon is
cooling, not collapsing: new 2026 sets fall back to near list price within
months and 30th Celebration singles fell 40% to 50%, while demand, events and
graded high-end cards hold up. Among the other games, One Piece holds its
sealed value best, Magic leads in sales and events but its new sealed product
sells below list price, and Star Wars: Unlimited is fading. For turnover, raw
singles in the $5 to $80 range, priced a little under market, move fastest;
slabs sell slower and grading only pays on chase cards worth about $200 or more
raw.

## Headline

1. **Sports cards: bigger pool, growth at the top end.** eBay sports singles
   were $1.36B in H1 2026, up 66.7% year over year; the growth is in vintage,
   one-of-ones and star rookies, while modern base rookies softened 30% to 40%
   (section 1).
2. **Trading card games: smaller pool, faster growth.** TCG and non-sport
   singles on eBay grew about +87% half over half against +40% for sports
   (estimate), and TCG is about 70% of all cards graded in 2026 (section 1).
3. **Pokémon is cooling, not collapsing.** New sets fall to 1.0x to 1.2x list
   price within months; demand indicators and hobby allocation stay tight
   (section 2).
4. **One Piece holds sealed value best; Magic sells most; Star Wars: Unlimited
   is declining** (section 3).
5. **Raw singles turn over fastest. Grade only chase cards** (sections 4 and 5).

## 1. Trading card games against sports cards

**Finding:** Sports singles are the larger dollar pool on eBay; trading card
games are growing faster and dominate grading.
**Confidence:** sourced (GemRate data via cllct, Athlon and Yardbarker; eBay
earnings calls as fact)
**Retrieved:** 2026-10-04

| Measure | Sports | TCG and non-sport | Source |
|---------|-------:|------------------:|--------|
| eBay singles, 2025 | $1.78B | $837M | cllct via Yahoo, 2026-01-16, <https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/more-2-6-billion-spent-154500186.html> |
| eBay singles transactions, 2025 | 42M+ | 19.5M+ | same |
| Average eBay single, 2025 | $39 | $43 | same |
| eBay singles, H1 2026 | $1.36B (+66.7% year over year) | about $0.96B ($2.32B total less sports; estimate) | Athlon via Yahoo, 2026-07-31, <https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/ebays-1-36-billion-sports-074629085.html> |
| Growth, H1 2026 against H2 2025 | +40% | about +87% (estimate) | derived from the rows above |
| Cards graded, 2025 | 10.0M (-12%) | 16.8M (+95%) | cllct via Yahoo, 2026-01-03, <https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/major-authenticators-graded-more-26-051500958.html> |
| Share of cards graded, March 2026 | about 30% | about 70% | SI, 2026-04-04, <https://www.si.com/collectibles/march-gemrate-report-psa-tcg-rookie-hype-drive-record-card-month> |
| PSA grading, July 2026 | 662K, flat year over year | up 81% year over year | SI, 2026-08-05, <https://www.si.com/collectibles/july-card-grading-hits-another-record-beckett-cgc-surging> |

eBay's own words (fact): in Q1 2026 "Sports trading card GMV growth accelerated
notably" and was a "larger contributor to GMV growth than Pokémon"
(<https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/04/29/ebay-ebay-q1-2026-earnings-call-transcript/>);
in Q2 2026 growth came "across all of the major sports and collectible card
game genres," and eBay Live GMV "grew roughly eightfold year over year"
(<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001065088/000106508826000174/exhibit991erebayq22026.htm>).
On Whatnot, sports cards are the No. 1 US category, followed by trading card
games (Sports Collectors Daily, search snippet only, sourced).

**Where sports growth sits:** record sales are at the top end. Heritage's
August 2026 Platinum Night realized $62,963,254, the largest sports auction
ever (fact, <https://atticcapital.com/sports-card-market-report/>); a Cooper
Flagg rookie patch autograph sold for $8.04M on 2026-09-25 (sourced, The Hobby
Wire, <https://www.thehobbywire.com/p/the-state-of-the-hobby-october-2026>).
Top eBay sellers by player in H1 2026 were Ohtani ($85.67M), Jordan ($68.89M),
Wembanyama ($50.57M) and Flagg ($34.02M) (search snippet, sourced).

**Where sports is weak:** modern base and mid-tier cards. Athlon reports
"price softening of 30–40% year-over-year" on base rookie cards of blue-chip
prospects and an estimated 429 million NBA cards printed (sourced, low
reliability, no stated method;
<https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/junk-wax-2-0-overproduction-212736062.html>).
Unlicensed Panini Prizm Basketball boxes fell from a $459.95 to $499.95 presell
to $249.95 to $274.95, about -45% (sourced, retailer listings). Licensed Topps
boxes resell above Topps.com prices (2026 Topps Update hobby $129.99, about
$200 secondary; Cardlines, 2026-09-23,
<https://cardlines.com/2026-topps-update-series-baseball/>), but that premium
goes to whoever buys at Topps.com prices, which a store without an account
cannot ([sports-cards.md](sports-cards.md), section 2).

**Soccer and hockey are the near-term openings in sports.** World Cup demand
lifted July 2026 eBay sports sales to $290M (sourced, snippet), and Panini
holds the FIFA license through 2030, with Fanatics taking it from 2031 (fact,
Sports Collectors Digest, 2026-05-07,
<https://sportscollectorsdigest.com/fanatics-topps-to-take-over-fifa-soccer-card-deal-from-panini>).
A Celebrini Young Guns 1/1 sold for $1.281M at Goldin, a non-Gretzky hockey
record (sourced, snippet), and Upper Deck hockey is the one licensed sports
line a new storefront may reach ([sports-cards.md](sports-cards.md),
section 2).

**Disconfirming check:** no 2026 Card Ladder index for any sport could be
opened (every page returned 403); the commonly repeated "-2% to -11%" sport
index figures date from November 2024 and are not used here.

## 2. Is Pokémon cracking?

**Finding:** The post-release premium is shrinking fast and hype-driven singles
fell hard, but demand, events and graded high-end cards hold up.
**Confidence:** sourced (PriceCharting monthly series; ICv2; Circana)
**Retrieved:** 2026-10-04

**New 2026 sets return toward list price** (booster box list price $161.64,
ETB $49.99, per ICv2 2026-08-28,
<https://icv2.com/articles/news/view/63244/pokemon-tcg-shows-signs-cooling-off-from-hot-summer-sales>):

| Product | Launch level | October 2026 | Change | Multiple of list price |
|---------|-------------:|-------------:|-------:|-----------------------:|
| Chaos Rising booster box | $240.00 (May) | $182.28 | -24% | 1.13x |
| Chaos Rising ETB | $94.84 (May) | $71.29 | -25% | 1.43x |
| Perfect Order booster box | $203.88 (Mar) | $164.07 | -20% | 1.02x |
| Pitch Black booster box | $204.69 (Jul) | $191.44 | -6% | 1.18x |

Source: PriceCharting monthly ungraded series (sourced; the page does not
document its method), e.g.
<https://www.pricecharting.com/game/pokemon-chaos-rising/booster-box>.
ICv2: "the profit margin for TCG sealed product holders seems to be steadily
deteriorating."

**Older sets rose through mid-2026 and turned down from about July:** Destined
Rivals box $254 at launch, $559 peak (Feb 2026), $407 now (-27% from peak);
Prismatic Evolutions ETB $176 peak (Mar 2026), $142 now, still 2.8x list price;
Surging Sparks box flat at $300 (sourced, PriceCharting).

**30th Celebration (Sept 2026):** Special Illustration singles fell 40% to 50%
and one Pikachu over 80%, blamed on "higher hit rates and an oversupply of
product for the initial wave" (sourced, ICv2, 2026-09-23,
<https://icv2.com/articles/news/view/63449/pokmon-tcg-flippers-meltdown-discord-wave-schadenfreude-washes-over-some-fans>).
Sealed has held: ETBs sold at $145 to $168 in the week to 2026-10-04, about
3x list price (sourced, PriceCharting).

**Demand has not broken:**
- Circana, 2026-08-12: US toy dollars +17% in H1 2026, Games and Puzzles +45%,
  "extraordinary performance of trading cards" (sourced,
  <https://www.circana.com/post/us-toy-industry-posts-strongest-first-half-in-six-years-circana-reports>).
- Delta Reign preorders at the Pokémon Center "sold out as fast as it was
  opened up" (sourced, ICv2, 2026-10-01,
  <https://icv2.com/articles/news/view/63514/pokemon-tcg-moves-right-on-mega-evolution-delta-reign>).
- Regional championships drew 2,270 (Toronto, Jan), 2,793 (Frankfurt, Sep) and
  3,122 (Baltimore, Sep) players (sourced, search snippets; no year-over-year
  comparison found).
- A Card Ladder Pokémon index rose 27% between May and August 2026 (search
  snippet only, sourced); this tracks mostly graded high-end cards, not new
  sealed product.

**Supply stays tight for hobby stores:** about 10 billion cards were printed in
the year to March 2026 (sourced, PokeGuardian citing The Pokémon Company), and
the new North Carolina printing plant does not start until late 2028 (sourced,
WRAL, 2025-12-18,
<https://www.wral.com/business/pokemon-card-maker-expands-north-carolina-factory-2025/>).
Hobby stores still get little: one distributor sent a store a single 30th
Celebration ETB ([distribution-and-supply.md](distribution-and-supply.md),
section 3.2).

**The one falling demand series:** Pokémon TCG Pocket, the digital game, fell
from 39M to 23M monthly users year over year (search snippet only, sourced).

**Disconfirming check:** no Bloomberg, WSJ, CNBC or cllct analysis of a Pokémon
correction could be read; the "correction" articles found came from price
tracker and SEO sites and are not used.

## 4. Slabs against raw cards

**Finding:** For most cards under about $100, raw sells better; grading pays
only on chase cards. Buying finished slabs is more liquid than submitting.
**Confidence:** sourced (PriceCharting, SI, ICv2, Cardlines); estimate for the
break-even arithmetic
**Retrieved:** 2026-10-04

**Raw modern Pokémon already prices like a PSA 9; the premium is all in the
10** (PriceCharting, sourced; multiples estimate):

| Card | Raw | PSA 9 | PSA 10 | PSA 10 / raw |
|------|----:|------:|-------:|-------------:|
| Pikachu ex 238, Surging Sparks | $254.45 | $251.79 | $837.50 | 3.3x |
| Charizard ex 199, 151 | $344.32 | $364.76 | $1,375.00 | 4.0x |
| Umbreon ex 161, Prismatic Evolutions | $1,179.50 | $1,255.71 | $5,287.50 | 4.5x |
| Umbreon VMAX 215, Evolving Skies (2021) | $1,790.73 | $2,119.61 | $3,875.00 | 2.2x |

**Grading costs and waits, October 2026:**

| Company | Cheapest open tier | Source |
|---------|-------------------|--------|
| PSA | Standard, $59.99, 90 to 100 business days (Value tiers paused since 2026-06-02; backlog 9.9M cards) | ICv2, 2026-09-22, <https://icv2.com/articles/news/view/63434/psa-adds-grading-service-tier-opens-psa-europe-facility> (sourced) |
| CGC | Bulk $17, Economy $20; $11 per card through Fanatics Collect | CGC, 2026-03-10, <https://www.cgcgrading.com/news/articles/cgc-updates-bulk-economy-tiers-39EPnFbgfEFcl9dg16hFnr/> (fact); <https://www.fanaticscollect.com/how-to-grade> (fact) |
| SGC | $50 per card from 2026-09-02 | SI, 2026-09-02 (sourced) |
| TAG | Value and Bulk paused; Base $39.95 and up | search snippet (sourced) |

"The only major grading company with a service under $50 per card is CGC"
(sourced, The Hobby Wire, 2026-10-02).

**Break-even, PSA Standard** (estimate; assumes 13% selling fees, $5 shipping
and insurance, and a 9 selling at the PSA 9 price): a Pikachu ex 238 bought raw
needs a 10 about 13% of the time; a Charizard ex 199 about 8%. A $30 card with
a $90 PSA 10 cannot break even at PSA's $60 tier at any gem rate, and needs
about 38% gems at CGC Bulk. Modern trading card games gem at about 60% (guess,
blog citing GemRate), so populations of PSA 10s grow fast and premiums
compress. Cardlines: the Standard tier makes "limited sense" for modern cards
worth $30 to $100 raw (sourced, 2026-09-24,
<https://cardlines.com/what-psas-new-standard-grading-tier-means-for-collectors/>).

**Sports slabs:** base modern rookie PSA 10s repriced down after grading fees
rose (2024 Bowman Elly De La Cruz PSA 10 from $60 to $42; sourced, Athlon via
Yahoo, 2026-05-28); slabs keep their value on rare parallels, short prints and
vintage, where authentication matters.

**Risks in slabs:**
- PSA intercepted more than $200M of counterfeit and altered items; Pokémon
  counterfeits rose 125% (sourced, SI, 2026-05-08,
  <https://www.si.com/collectibles/psa-report-reveals-200m-fake-cards>). Fake
  slabs reuse real certificate numbers.
- PSA, SGC and Beckett now share one parent, Collectors, after its Beckett deal
  (sourced, 2025-12-15).
- Pokémon reportedly bars partnered vendors at official events from selling
  slabs or items over $1,000, from 2026-05-29 (sourced, leaked memo reported by
  PokeBeach and Nerdbeak).

**Disconfirming check:** no credible days-to-sell comparison between graded and
raw cards was found; one analytics site's sell-through figures contradicted
themselves across its own pages and are not used.

## 5. What turns over fastest

**Finding:** Raw singles in the $5 to $80 range turn fastest, at shows and in
store; card shows are the fastest channel in 2026; live selling works for
sellers who stream often.
**Confidence:** sourced (trade press, platform blogs, named dealers); guess
where marked
**Retrieved:** 2026-10-04

- **Cheap singles move the most copies.** The top-selling Pokémon cards by
  copies on TCGplayer in July 2026 averaged $1.21 to $3.02 (fact, TCGplayer,
  2026-08-05,
  <https://seller.tcgplayer.com/blog/top-selling-pok%C3%A9mon-cards-on-tcgplayer-july-2026>),
  but at that price fees and postage take most of the margin
  ([online-and-legal.md](online-and-legal.md), section 1.8).
- **The working band is $5 to $80.** A Pokémon show-vending guide says
  singles at "$5–$80 range move quickest," "graded cards … move slower than raw
  singles," and to price "5–10% below TCGPlayer market" (sourced, vendor blog,
  2026-05-27,
  <https://tistaminis.com/blogs/blog/vending-at-pokemon-shows-a-beginner-s-guide>).
  Dealers describe dollar and quarter boxes as traffic: "the cheap stuff moves"
  (guess, snippet).
- **Card shows sell out.** The 2026 National drew more than 100,000 people and
  1,000-plus dealers; one dealer "ran out of cards on Saturday," another sold
  over 100 of 300 vintage Mantles, and buying inventory, not selling it, was the
  constraint (sourced, Sports Collectors Digest, 2026-08-10,
  <https://sportscollectorsdigest.com/longtime-sports-card-dealers-in-awe-over-growth-of-the-national-record-sales>).
- **Live selling rewards frequency.** Whatnot says daily streamers earn 100 to
  250 times more than monthly ones and sellers average 23 hours a week (sourced,
  Whatnot's 2026 Live Selling Report via Value Added Resource,
  <https://www.valueaddedresource.net/whatnot-2026-live-selling-report/>).
  eBay Live charges trading cards 8.9% and $0.30 per order (sourced,
  2026-07-18, <https://www.valueaddedresource.net/new-ebay-live-fee-rates/>),
  below Whatnot's 8% commission and 2.9% + $0.30 processing. A $1 starting bid is a format, not a
  strategy: on a $3 sale, Whatnot's fees take about 21% before shipping
  (estimate).
- **The biggest eBay card seller averages about $31 a sale** across 1.4 million
  transactions from January to September 2026 (guess, snippet; estimate).
- **Avoid repacks and mystery boxes:** pending arbitration against Whatnot and
  Fanatics Live and California's Penal Code 319.3 target them (sourced, Womble
  Bond Dickinson, 2026-08-05).

**Release calendar, Q4 2026:** Pokémon Delta Reign 2026-11-06 with prereleases
from 2026-10-24 (fact, ICv2 calendar); the last 30th Celebration wave
2026-12-04; Riftbound Radiance 2026-10-23; Topps Flagship Basketball preorders
2026-10-06 and Panini Select Basketball 2026-10-14 (sourced, snippet). eBay
singles in December 2025 ran 11% (sports) and 23% (TCG) above that year's
monthly average (estimate).

## What this means for the store

These points are interpretation, built on the sections above:

1. **Keep the store TCG-led, with sports as a real singles line.** TCG is
   growing faster and carries the events, while sports singles are a large,
   liquid pool on eBay and Whatnot; the
   [storefront plan](../storefront-plan.md) sets its mix line by line on that
   basis. Sports money comes from singles, graded
   rookies and vintage bought from the public, not from modern sealed boxes.
2. **Tier the games by what each does best.** One Piece for sealed and events;
   Magic for singles turnover and events, buying sealed only to event demand;
   Pokémon for traffic, singles and League nights, with sealed as allocated;
   Riftbound and Lorcana in measured amounts given reprints and speculation;
   Yu-Gi-Oh! for cheap weekly events and singles; Star Wars: Unlimited and
   Flesh and Blood on demand only.
3. **Make raw singles the core and treat slabs as a showcase.** Buy finished
   slabs at a discount for the case and for eBay; grade only modern chase cards
   worth $200 or more raw, pre-screened, and prefer CGC on price; check every
   slab against counterfeits.
4. **Do not hold new Pokémon or Magic sealed product as an investment.** Sell
   it through to players at or near market; the premium fades within months.
5. **Use shows and live selling for turnover.** Shows sell out in 2026, and
   regular eBay Live or Whatnot streams move the $5 to $80 band.
