# Storefront Plan on $50,000

> Last updated: 2026-10-01
> Status: first draft. Built from the six research files. No quote, count or
> sales figure in it has been collected yet; the gates in section 2 exist to
> collect them.

A singles-first card store with a 20-seat play area in an 858 sq ft bay, run by
the owner with a helper on two event nights, opened in stages. Every figure
carries a confidence tier as defined on the [overview](README.md). Figures
marked "plan" are choices this plan makes, not findings.

## Headline, pessimistic first

1. **$50,000 opens this store with $518 to spare, and only in the small bay.**
   Setup and six months of operating cash leave $12,721 for opening inventory.
   Break-even sales need $12,203 of inventory at 5 turns a year (section 6,
   estimate built on guesses). Any overrun above $518 leaves stock too thin to
   reach break-even. The larger Suite 1603 needs about $59,900 (section 9).
2. **The store needs $7,567 a month in sales to cover its costs and $18,274 to
   pay the owner $3,000 a month** (section 5, estimate built on guesses). The
   second figure is about 72% of the trade area's estimated hobby TCG spend at
   the 2025 peak, and more than all of it at the 2024 level (guess).
3. **5 turns a year is optimistic for this margin.** The one store owner whose
   rule the research records says a margin under 40% needs about ten turns
   ([category and unit economics](research/category-and-unit-economics.md),
   section 3, guess). This plan's margin is 32.8% before shrink and card fees.
4. **The owner draws nothing from the store in year one.** Owner living costs
   and health insurance sit outside the $50,000; gate G6 requires them to be
   funded for 24 months.
5. **No lease is signed until eight gates pass** (section 2). The ones the plan
   rests on are local player count (G2), buying cards below market (G3) and
   evidence of sales (G7).

## 2. Gates before signing a lease

The thresholds are plan choices tied to the arithmetic below, not sourced
benchmarks. Gates G1, G5 and G6 cost nothing and run first.

| # | Gate | Passes when | Fails means |
|---|------|-------------|-------------|
| G1 | Distributor cost and supply | A written or named-contact quote from GTS (800-890-5456), Southern Hobby or PHD gives at least 30% margin at TCGplayer market on in-print Pokémon, Magic, One Piece and Lorcana sealed, and states what a new account receives on in-print Pokémon. If no distributor quotes before a lease, an existing store owner's invoice stands in | Sealed becomes an event and traffic item only; rerun section 5. 30% leaves a 3-point cushion over the 27% the model uses |
| G2 | Local players | At least 20 unique Michigan City residents logged (initials and ZIP) across events at Reliquary, High Heat and Heroes Haven and the library night (section 7), at least 8 of them playing Pokémon or One Piece; each asked their monthly card spend | No lease. 20 is the seat count (section 3) |
| G3 | Buying below market | From Nov 1 to Feb 28, at least $5,000 at cost bought from the public at 60% of TCGplayer market or less, cards under $0.50 counted at $0. By Apr 15, at least half the cost basis sold, at 40% or more on in-person sales (sale price less cost) and 18% or more on marketplace sales after fees, postage and supplies, logged per card | No lease. The main line does not work |
| G4 | Cost quotes | Written quotes for build-out (including whether the restroom is accessible as-is), security to the insurer's specification, sign, and insurance (inventory limit at least the opening inventory, burglary covered, collectibles not excluded) leave opening inventory at or above $12,203 | Re-budget. An overrun comes straight out of inventory |
| G5 | Code, zoning and lease | Planning (219-873-1419) confirms retail with events is allowed in the bay; the building department (219-873-1417) confirms one exit serves the planned load and names the permit path and fire-inspection items. Lease terms: rent-free build-out to opening, renewal options, personal guarantee capped at 6 to 12 months of rent, event and after-hours use permitted, the landlord's prior-year NNN statement | Different bay, or renegotiate |
| G6 | Owner runway | The owner documents personal funds or household income covering living costs and individual health insurance for 24 months from Oct 2026, with the amount stated | No lease |
| G7 | Sales evidence | A bottom-up estimate from G2 (players x stated monthly spend), or a comparable store's monthly sales totals, reaches $7,567 a month by month 6 | No lease |
| G8 | Capital | Cash on hand covers setup, six months of operating cost and the $12,203 inventory floor after the G4 quotes | Raise capital or cut a line before signing |

**After opening:** if trailing three-month sales sit below $5,675 a month (75%
of break-even) at month 9, stop buying and begin wind-down (plan).

**Market size check** (guess). US and Canada hobby TCG sales of about $2.44B in
2025 ([category and unit economics](research/category-and-unit-economics.md),
section 1, estimate) over about 383 million people (population figure not
sourced in this repo) is about $6.37 per person. The core trade area holds
50,625 residents, 2,854 of them in group quarters in ZCTA 46360, likely the
state prison ([storefront costs](research/storefront-costs-and-demand.md),
section 1). The other 47,771 come to about $304,000 a year across all hobby
channels. Break-even is 30% of that and the $3,000 pay level 72%. 2025 was a
growth spike; at the 2024 level (about $1.44B nationally) the local figure is
about $180,000, and the $3,000 pay level exceeds it. Whether ICv2's hobby
channel figure includes online hobby sales is not stated in the research.

## 6. Inventory and cash

At 5 turns a year and a 67.2% cost ratio (1 - 32.8%), sales of $7,567 a month
need 7,567 x 12 x 0.672 / 5 = $12,203 of inventory at cost (estimate; the
5-turn figure is a plan assumption). Opening inventory of $12,721 supports
5 x 12,721 / 0.672 / 12 = $7,888 a month, $321 above break-even sales and $90
a month of margin over costs.
Paying the owner $3,000 a month needs about $29,500 of inventory at the same
turn rate. Gross profit cannot fund that gap at break-even volume; owner pay
waits on sales rising first.

The operating cash covers six months of costs with zero sales. The build-out
months are paid from the pre-opening occupancy line in section 4, so the six
months start at opening. A rent-free build-out (gate G5) returns $1,358 to
inventory and raises the room above the floor to $1,876.

## 9. The larger bay

1601 Franklin St, Suite 1603, 1,370 sq ft at $7.00 asking ($1,142 a month
all-in with $3 NNN, estimate), fits the research's space range and a larger
sales floor. With a helper on three event nights (12 hours a week, $803 a
month), monthly cost is $2,850, break-even $10,172 a month, and opening
inventory $6,489 against a $16,405 floor. It needs about $59,900 in capital
without a rent-free build-out, $57,600 with one (estimate).

## 10. Risks

| Risk | Size | What the plan does |
|------|------|--------------------|
| Cost overrun | $518 of room above the inventory floor; build-out alone runs $5,000 to $25,000 in the research | G4 and G8; choose a bay with an accessible restroom; negotiate a rent-free build-out |
| Theft and burglary | $5,000 (distraction theft, Chesterton) to $340,000 (burglary, 90 miles away) per incident | Singles and high-value stock to the safe overnight; insurer's security specification; collectibles theft limit in the policy (G4) |
| Singles lose value | 30th Celebration Special Illustration singles fell 40% to 50%, one card over 80% | Buy chase cards of a new set only after its first four weeks; cap any single card at 5% of singles inventory (plan) |
| Sealed loses value | Pokémon boxes and ETBs fell 17% to 24% within two months of release; OP-13 fell 36%; one Magic set fell below cost within days | Sealed held to $5,089 at opening; no speculative stock |
| Allocation | New stores start near zero on popular sets | Singles-first mix; TCGplayer-sourced sealed capped |
| Competition | Reliquary's events 11.0 mi away; the monthly 100-table FOP show in town; Heroes Haven could add Pokémon or One Piece | G2 and G7 measure it before a lease |
| Counterfeit and stolen cards in public buys | No rate found; Bandai warns of counterfeits at event venues | ID and log every seller; refuse sealed product from individuals; refuse when unsure (plan) |
| Owner income and key person | $0 pay at break-even; the store closes when the owner cannot work | G6 funds 24 months of living costs |
| Lease after failure | $24,444 over a 3-year term | G5 caps any personal guarantee; wind-down rule in section 2 |
| Category downturn | 2025 was a growth spike; the 2024 base is about 40% lower | G7 uses current local spend, not the national trend |
| Program rejection | Lorcana HSP acceptance limited; Pokémon requires current sealed product | Apply after build-out; fallback events in unsanctioned play |
