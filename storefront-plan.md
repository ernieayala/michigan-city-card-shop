# Storefront Plan on $50,000

> Last updated: 2026-09-30
> Status: first draft. Built from the six research files. No quote, count or
> sales figure in it has been collected yet; the pre-lease gates in section 2
> exist to collect them.

A singles-first card store with a 20-seat play area, run by the owner with a
part-time event helper, opened in stages. Every figure carries a confidence tier
as defined on the [overview](README.md). Figures marked "plan" are choices this
plan makes, not findings.

## Headline, pessimistic first

1. **$50,000 opens the store only at the low end of every cost line.** Setup,
   six months of operating cost and opening inventory total $50,000 with $0
   contingency (section 4). Any overrun comes out of inventory. Build-out is the
   largest risk: $5,000 assumes a bay with a compliant restroom; the research
   range runs to $25,000.
2. **The store needs about $7,600 a month in sales to cover its costs and about
   $16,900 to pay the owner $3,000 a month** (section 5, estimate). The second
   figure is roughly 63% of the core trade area's estimated hobby TCG spend
   (guess).
3. **The owner draws nothing from the store in year one.** Six months of
   operating cost is held as cash; owner living costs are not in the $50,000.
4. **Opening inventory of $14,582 supports about $8,900 a month in sales** at 5
   turns a year. Growth past that is funded from gross profit, which competes
   with owner pay (section 6, estimate).
5. **No lease is signed until five gates pass** (section 2). Two of them, local
   player count and buying cards below market, are the tests the whole plan
   rests on.

| Line | Monthly | Confidence |
|------|--------:|------------|
| Rent, all-in (section 3) | $1,142 | estimate |
| Electric, gas, water | $250 | guess |
| Internet | $80 | guess |

| Workers comp | $86 | sourced |
| Event helper, 8 hr/wk at $14.35 with 7.65% employer FICA | $536 | estimate |
| **Total** | **$2,418** | estimate |

Event helper arithmetic: 8 x $14.35 x 52 / 12 x 1.0765 = $535.52. The wage is
the BLS May 2025 retail median for the Michigan City-La Porte MSA (fact).

**Blended gross margin: 32%** (estimate built on guesses). Sales mix 45%
singles at 45%, 40% sealed at 27%, 10% accessories at 30%, 5% events at 20%:
0.45 x 45% + 0.40 x 27% + 0.10 x 30% + 0.05 x 20% = 35.1%, less about 2.6% card
processing (Square Plus, 2.5% + 15¢, fact) = 32%. The mix, the accessory margin
and the event margin are guesses.

| Owner pay | Gross profit needed | Sales needed per month | Per year |
|-----------|--------------------:|-----------------------:|---------:|
| $0 | $2,418 | $7,556 | ~$90,700 |
| $3,000/mo | $5,418 | $16,931 | ~$203,000 |
| $21.75/hr x 50 hr/wk = $4,713/mo (BLS retail supervisor median) | $7,131 | $22,283 | ~$267,000 |

**What the owner earns at a given sales level** (sales x 32% - $2,418):

| Monthly sales | Gross profit | Owner earnings |
|--------------:|-------------:|---------------:|
| $8,000 | $2,560 | $142 |
| $12,000 | $3,840 | $1,422 |
| $17,000 | $5,440 | $3,022 |
| $22,000 | $7,040 | $4,622 |

**Market size check** (guess). US and Canada hobby TCG sales of about $2.44B in
2025 ([category and unit economics](research/category-and-unit-economics.md),
section 1, estimate) over about 383 million people (population figure not
sourced in this repo) is about $6.37 per person. The core trade area's 50,625
residents come to about $323,000 a year across all hobby channels.
$203,000 is 63% of that; $90,700 is 28%. Michigan City's median household income
sits below the county's, and part of the national figure is online hobby
spending, so the local figure is more likely high than low.

## 6. Inventory and cash

At 5 turns a year and 32% margin, $14,582 of inventory at cost supports
5 x $14,582 / 0.68 = $107,221 in yearly sales, about $8,900 a month (estimate;
the 5-turn rule is one store owner's, guess). That clears break-even with no
owner pay. Reaching $16,931 a month needs $16,931 x 12 x 0.68 / 5 = about
$27,600 of inventory at the same turn rate, so year-one gross profit goes back into stock before it goes to
the owner.

The operating cash covers six months of costs with zero gross profit. At $8,000
a month in sales the store covers its costs and the cash is not drawn.

## 9. Risks

| Risk | Size | What the plan does |
|------|------|--------------------|
| Build-out overrun | $5,000 budget against a $25,000 research high | Gate G4; choose a bay with a compliant restroom |
| Burglary | $5,000 to $340,000 per incident in the region | Singles and high-value stock to the safe overnight; insurance quote with a stated collectibles theft limit (G4) |
| Sealed product loses value | 30th Celebration singles fell 40% to 80% in a week | Sealed held to $6,082 at opening; no speculative stock |
| Allocation | New stores start near zero on popular sets | Singles-first mix; TCGplayer-sourced sealed capped |
| Owner income | $0 at $7,556 a month in sales | Owner living costs come from outside the store in year one |
| Local demand too small | Break-even with pay needs about 63% of estimated local spend | Gates G2 and G3 before any lease |
